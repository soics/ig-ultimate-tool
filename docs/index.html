    How do I get my Instagram data file?
    1. In the Instagram app, open your profile, tap the menu, then Accounts Center.
    2. Tap Your information and permissions > Download your information > Download or transfer information.
    3. Pick your account, choose Some of your information, tick Followers and following.
    4. Choose Download to device, format JSON, then Create files.
    5. Instagram emails you when it's ready (often within an hour). Drop the .zip here.

    Menu names change now and then; search the app's settings for "Download your information".

      What it does with your account, honestly.

      What stays private

      • It runs on your computer and talks only to Instagram, plus the AI you pick if you turn auto-reply on.
      • Your password is typed hidden and never saved. Only Instagram's login session is kept, readable by you alone.
      • No accounts, no tracking, no servers of ours. Every line of code is public on GitHub.

      What can go wrong

      • Automating an account is against Instagram's terms. Instagram can pause actions for a day or two ("Action Blocked"); the tool stops by itself when that happens.
      • Older, real accounts are far lower risk than brand-new ones. Fast mode is faster and riskier.
      • Deleted posts, chats and comments don't come back. Download your data first.

      Is this made by Instagram?

      No. It's an independent open-source project, not affiliated with Instagram or Meta.

      Why not just use a website that does this?

      Websites that log into your Instagram hold your account on their servers. This tool never leaves your computer, and you can read every line of it.

      Will I get banned?

      Very unlikely on an aged, real account at the normal speed. You may see Instagram's temporary "Action Blocked" after lots of actions; the tool stops, and continues where it left off after you wait a day or two.

      Does it work with two-factor login?

      Yes, it asks for your code. If Instagram asks "Was this you?", approve it in the app, wait 15 minutes, and try again.

      What does the checker do with my file?

      It opens the file in your browser's memory, compares your followers and following, and shows the result. The page's security settings block it from uploading or sending data anywhere. If you drop a .zip, a small zip reader is downloaded from cdnjs to open it.
